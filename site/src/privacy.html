Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DDO Gear Planner (ddo-gear-planner.com) ("the Site," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our DDO gear planner.

Information We Collect

Account information (via Google or Discord Sign-In): If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name, and profile picture to create and identify your account. If you sign in with Discord, we receive your email address and username. We don't request access to anything else in your Google or Discord account.

Saved build data: If you create an account, we store the gear builds you save, associated with your account.

Analytics: We use Google Analytics, which uses cookies, to understand how visitors use the Site. This data is aggregated and not tied to your account identity. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

How We Use Information

We use this information to authenticate you, save/display your builds, and understand aggregate usage of the Site. We do not sell your information or share it with third parties except as required by law.

Data Deletion

To request deletion of your account and data, contact us at privacy@ddo-gear-planner.com.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy: privacy@ddo-gear-planner.com

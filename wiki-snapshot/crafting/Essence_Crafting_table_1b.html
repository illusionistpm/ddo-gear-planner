This table lists all prefix, suffix, and extra effects that can be added on to different gear via Essence Crafting.

Notes:

  • Extra effects can be applied to items ML 10 and higher only.
  • Effects that grant insight bonuses can be applied to items ML 10 and higher only, regardless of prefix/suffix/extra slot.
    That includes "Parrying" (which grants an insight bonus). Note that an effect named "Insightful Parrying" does not currently exist.
  • See also: Compiled listing of all Essence Crafting Tables

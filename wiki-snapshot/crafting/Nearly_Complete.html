Nearly Completed is a crafting mechanic that allows players to upgrade items from the Terror of Demogorgon expansion with an extra enchantment from a small list at the Duergar Completion Forge in Gravenhollow.

Which set of enchantments is available for an item depends on which version of the Nearly Complete enchantment the item has. For example, an item with Nearly Complete: Ability ScoreNearly Complete: Ability Score: This item isn't quite finished, but it's only a step away from completion. Bring it to the forges on the upper floor of Gravenhollow and combine it with melted materials to restore this item to its full potential. can be upgraded with an Enhancement bonus to one of the core stats. Upgrading an item with Nearly Completed requires 25 Abyssal Gems (or Legendary Abyssal Gems for Legendary items) from Terror of Demogorgon end chests. Note that you cannot replace a Nearly Completed upgrade after selecting one - you will need a new copy of the item to apply a different effect.

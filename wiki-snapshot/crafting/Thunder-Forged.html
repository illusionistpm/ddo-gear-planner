Crafting device[edit]

Magma Forge

  • Located within the Ruins of Thunderholme, lower area
  • A fast travel NPC in the Thunder Peaks public area will take you to the forge if you have defeated the Dragon Emissary (Sarva Bellistrae) flagging yourself for the Temple of the Deathwyrm raid. (No Astral Shard usage required.)

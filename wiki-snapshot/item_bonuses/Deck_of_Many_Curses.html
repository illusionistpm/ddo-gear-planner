  • Name: Deck of Many Curses
  • Turn-In: 12 Accursed Cards, 12 Uncanny Cards, 12 Otherworldly Cards, and 12 Forbidden Cards
  • Minimum Level: 1
  • Binding: Bound to Account on Acquire
  • Effect: Deck allows you to place a permanent, random, positive enchantment on an item.
  • Base Value: 000001000010ppPlatinum Piece 
  • Stack size: 1
  • Notes: Existing enchantment can be removed using Card of Curse Cleansing.

About cards[edit]

Twelve cards of each type, 48 in all, are required to create a Deck. Cards are unbound, and might be found in the Auction House and Shard Exchange.

Cards drop in the Illithid Invasion content, in Legendary Sagas, and may appear as rewards in later content as well.

Illithid Invasion[edit]

All four types of card appear in each quest, in both the end chests and one optional chest per quest.

A Festivult Curse![edit]

In 2023's run of Festivult (Update 65), the natural drop rate for cards in the four Illithid Invasion quests was doubled, and stacks of cards were added to the reward lists of Legendary Sagas. During that run of Festivult, stacks of 6 cards were also available as rewards for turning in Gold coins to the Jester of Festivult. This has since become standard for future runs of Festivult.

About curses[edit]

Despite the name, "curses" are generally positive.

  • Random: You cannot affect which curse you get. (You may be "cursed" with something irrelevant to the character, but rarely detrimental.)
  • Permanent: They cannot be removed - except using the extremely rare Card of Curse Cleansing. Also crafting upgrades that effectively destroy the old item and create a brand new one (such as Epic Crafting) remove the curse.
    • Because of this, you can effectively freely reroll Epic items for free using the Altar of Epic Rituals. Placing an upgradeable Epic item into this machine will recreate the item from scratch, destroying any attached augments---but also removing any Curses.
  • Curses grant Fortune bonus. Multiple Fortune bonuses to the same statistic do not stack.
  • Curses don't affect item binding or minimum level.
  • Eligible items: named, random, crafted, ...
  • Ineligible items: Augments, Cosmetics, Wands, Scrolls, Quivers, or Ammunition
    • This means that characters can have a maximum of 14 curses equipped at one time (13 if holding a two-handed weapon.)

Effects removed during Lamannia preview[edit]

  • Curse of Stone (weight/sinking), as its effects were largely negative
  • Curse of Wealth (extra weight and plat value), as it was folded into the Curse of the Sentient's Meal
  • Curse of Superior Augmentation (Augment Slot), as it could not be safely removed with the Card of Curse Cleansing
  • Curse of the Mythical Blessing (Mythic bonus), as it broke the entire stacking system
  • Curse of the Deckmaster (set bonus), as players pointed out the potential impact on items with relatively malleable set bonuses, and it could not be safely removed with the Card of Curse Cleansing
  • Curse of Minor/Major Intuition (lowers item's minimum level); no reason was given, but chances are it could not be safely removed with the Card of Curse Cleansing, and it might have interfered with the Sentient XP system
  • the Threat Boost/Reduction elements of all Curses that originally included them (Anger's Heart and Charming Presence), as both could be negative to some characters

This is a list of known Wands by spell level found in the game as random loot or available from vendors.

All of these wands cast the spell listed at the minimum possible caster level and ability score, unless otherwise specified. Wands found in random loot are generated with a random amount of charges (max 50); wands purchased from vendors always have their full 50 charges. After the last charge is used the wand is destroyed.

Availability legend[edit]

  • Green checkmark sources:
    • Market - The Marketplace Bazaar (or The Rusty Nail tavern)
    • House J - House Jorasco, House of Wizardry
    • House P - House Phiarlan, Object Desire
    • Twelve - The Twelve, In the Runes
    • House K - House Kundarak, Mona Harper Guild Wand Vendor (Minimum Guild Level 22, Guild Vendor wands are Bound to Account)
  • No checkmarks: available only as random loot
    • Bold: particularly useful but not available from vendors

Adventure packs are collections of quests that are connected by theme, location and/or story line. Some of the content can be played individually, while some forms a chain that can or must be run together, often for a special end-reward. VIPs have access to most Adventure packs, except those contained within an Expansion pack.

Adventure packs are purchased individually from the DDO Store for an account, using DDO Points, and are server wide for that account. (Guest Passes are also available for most adventure packs, but the buyer must already own that pack to purchase a guest pass.)

When choosing an adventure pack, you may want to consider:

  • A source of XP: Some packs provide better XP than F2P quests.
  • Named Loot: Newer packs tend to have better loot, and some packs offer their own crafting system. But some older packs have classic items.
  • Fun and variety: Do you need a change of pace from what you currently run?
  • Popularity: How often do you see LFM's for these quests in the Social Panel, or in your Guild?
  • Favor*: Total and/or specific Patron.
  • Sagas: Some packs include a Saga, some don't, some are only part of one.
  • Timing: It's generally better to buy a pack just over your current level, so you can get full experience from it, instead of waiting until you reincarnate. This may not be true if you're just buying a pack for Favor.
  • Sales: Weekly DDO Sales start every Thursday, are usually 20-30% off, and rarely as high as 50%. Not all weekly sales contain sales on Adventure Packs. Sales are announced 🗄 in the forums 🗄in the forums

Quest Bundles[edit]

Introduced in Update 73, Quest Bundles are a variant of adventure packs that use slightly different rules:

  • The requirement that quests in a pass share a central theming, story, setting, patron or level range is relaxed. The first pass had quests in a variety of locations with several different level pages, and even had Eberron and Forgotten Realms quests sold together.
  • Quest Bundles are not released all at once - instead they are drip-released over a single update, similar to Illithid Invasion without the unlock requirements.
  • Quests in a Quest Bundle can be bought from the DDO Store individually, for a slightly higher total cost than buying the pass. Buying a quest does not discount the cost of the pass in future. VIPs gain access to all Quest Bundles as with Adventure Packs.

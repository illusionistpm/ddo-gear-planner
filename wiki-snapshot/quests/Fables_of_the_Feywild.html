Journey to the land of myth and fable, a place of wonder and death in Dungeons & Dragons Online: Fables of the Feywild. Meet the Archfey, mighty beings who may help or hinder you on your adventures. Encounter majestic Unicorns, mischievous Pixies, deadly Displacer Beasts, and fierce Fomorians in this all new expansion!

Fables of the Feywild is DDO's 5th expansion, introduced in Update 48 released November 5, 2020. DDO Forums. It forms part 2 of a story that began in The Lost Gatekeepers and is continued in Isle of Dread.

Features[edit]

  • Locations: The Feywild wilderness
  • Race: Shifter capable of temporarily embracing their bestial aspects to rend enemies and survive
  • Iconic Hero: Razorclaw Shifter a barbarian fighting with handwraps
  • Universal Enhancement Tree: Feydark Illusionist
  • Loot: Update 48 named items
  • Monsters: Displacer Beast, Redcaps, Unicorns, Archfey, Fomorian

Adventure pack[edit]

  • Fables of the Feywild Part One: Lord Arden's Slumber a 5-quest story arc (level 5/32)
  • Fables of the Feywild Part Two: Hyrsam and the Needle a 5-quest story arc (level 6/32)
  • The Feywild: 3 Wilderness Quests (5 - 6/32) set in the The Feywild wilderness
  • Raid: The Dryad and the Demigod
  • Saga: Fables of the Feywild (Heroic) & Fables of the Feywild (Legendary)

Public zone[edit]

  • Wynwood Hall

Wilderness adventure areas[edit]

  • The Feywild, a level 5/32 wilderness adventure area

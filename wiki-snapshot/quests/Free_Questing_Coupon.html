Free Questing Coupon

The Free Questing Coupon is a special coupon code used through Redeem Code in the DDO Store in-game to get a selected collection of adventure packs for free (permanently).

So far available:

  • DDOFREEQUESTS (Available from: June 3‍–‍August 31, 2020) Friends Forever Archived: DDOfreequests
  • DDOQUESTS2021 (Available from: October 14‍–‍December 31, 2021) Archived: DDOQUESTS2021
  • DUNGEONCRAWL (Available from: March 23‍–‍April 23, 2023) DDO Free Dungeoncrawl Archived: DUNGEONCRAWL
  • FREEDDO2024 (Available from: January 10‍–‍February 11, 2024) Producer letter/January 2024 Archived: FREEDDO2024
  • GATHERYOURPARTY2025 (Available from: October 2‍–‍December 31, 2025) Archived: GATHERYOURPARTY2025
    • Alternative social media code: LETSPARTY2025 (Available from: October 2‍–‍December 31, 2025)DDOunlimited
  • NATURAL20 (Available from: March 6‍–‍April 7, 2026) Archived: NATURAL20 (Archived: extension)
  • BIGBYISBETTER (Available from: October 14‍–‍November 10, 2026) Archived: Bigby's Better Gift

Magic of Myth Drannor

Venture into the heart of the Forgotten Realms and brave the perilous ruins of Myth Drannor. Harness the power of chaotic Wild Magic, navigate mysteries of the Forgotten Realms, and thwart the sinister Red Wizards of Thay in their quest for dark secrets. Join the adventure to restore the Elves' ancient capital—will you rise to the challenge? Dive into Magic of Myth Drannor and shape the future of the legendary Elven city!

Magic of Myth Drannor is DDO’s 9th expansion introduced in Update 69 and released August 14th, 2024.

Features[edit]

  • Locations: Myth Drannor (wilderness)
  • Level cap: increase to 34
  • Race: Eladrin
  • Iconic Hero: Eladrin Chaosmancer
  • Archetype: Wild Mage a Sorcerer archetype
  • Loot: Update 69 named items
  • Monsters: Naga, Imp, Fang Dragon, Hook Horror, Carrion Crawler

Adventure pack[edit]

  • Magic of Myth Drannor Part One: a 4-quest story arc (level 13/35)
  • Magic of Myth Drannor Part Two: a 5-quest story arc (13/35)
  • Ruins of Myth Drannor: 3 Wilderness Quests (13/35)
  • 1 side quest (13/35)
  • Raid: Threats Old and New
  • Saga: Magic of Myth Drannor (Heroic) & Magic of Myth Drannor (Legendary)

Public zone[edit]

  • Outskirts of Myth Drannor

Wilderness adventure area[edit]

  • Ruins of Myth Drannor, a level 13/35 wilderness adventure area

Quests[edit]

Explore the ruins of Myth Drannor and interfere with the plans of the Red Wizards of Thay.

Magic of Myth Drannor can be bought bundled with other features for cash money (3 different options available, as shown) in the DDO Market, or the Adventure Pack (adventures only) and/or many of those "other features", all separately, can be bought using DDO Points.

Talk to Naarhesh to claim your bonus items (located outside the entrance of the Outskirts of Myth Drannor in Eveningstar ) or in the Eberron Hall of Heroes

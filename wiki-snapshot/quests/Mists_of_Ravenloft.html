Dungeons & Dragons Online: Mists of Ravenloft expansion will immerse you in one of the most infamous D&D adventure settings. Save the people of Barovia, slay Strahd, and restore balance to the land. The stakes have never been higher! Mists of Ravenloft is DDO's third expansion, introduced in Update 37, released December 6, 2017.

Features[edit]

  • Locations: The Land of Barovia wilderness
  • Race: Aasimar
  • Iconic Race: Aasimar Scourge a Aasimar Ranger
  • Universal Enhancement Tree: Vistani Knife Fighter enhancements
  • Loot: Sentient Weapons - upgrade system for awakening sentience in ML20+ named weapons. Update 37 named items

Adventure pack[edit]

  • Intro quest (level 10/31)
  • In the Shadow of the Castle a 3-quest story arc (level 10/31-32)
  • The Vampire Hunters a 4-quest story arc (level 11/31)
  • The Light of the Land a 4-quest story arc (level 12/31-32)
  • Raid: The Curse of Strahd, and Old Baba's Hut (level 32)
  • Saga: Mists of Ravenloft (Heroic) & Mists of Ravenloft (Epic)

Public zone[edit]

  • Blood on the Vine Tavern, Blue Water Inn, and van Richten's Tower

Wilderness adventure area[edit]

  • The Land of Barovia , a level 10/30 wilderness adventure area

Quests[edit]

The Mists of Ravenloft adventure pack consists of 12 quests (Level 10-12, Epic 31) plus 2 raids (Epic 32 only). The series begins with one stand-alone quest and three story arcs. The story arcs have to be played in the order listed below. The final quest in each of the story arcs requires that you complete the previous quests (in any order). You get a reward (random loot) for completing each story arc.

Bundles and prices[edit]

Mists of Ravenloft can be bought as part of the DDO Expansion Trove for cash money at the DDO Market, or the Adventure Pack can be bought using DDO Points.

The following items were part of the original (now retired) bundles. Also available since December 2025 are the Escape to Ravenloft bundles, which are four DDO Store exclusive bundles containing various items that were originally available from purchasing the enhanced editions of Mists of Ravenloft before their removal from the DDO Market. (Note that the Utility Collection and Complete Collections both include all items in the previous list, excepting the Ravenloft expansion itself; the Complete Collection also includes everything in the Barovian Aristocrat and Vampire Hunter bundles.)

Timeline[edit]

  • Level 10 Veteran Status was made available for 10 days at the launch of the expansion to all accounts (whether or not they own the expansion) for all new characters.
  • Mists of Ravenloft released December 6, 2017
  • As of March 8th 2018, the quests (only) became available in the DDO Store.
  • November 26, 2025, Escape to Ravenloft bundles release with items from the Mists of Ravenloft expansion.

Overview[edit]

The purpose of this Guide is to list all quests, grouping them under either Free to Play or by Adventure Pack. The adventures are then grouped by city zone (if applicable), and further broken down by quest level, favor & corresponding patron, experience, and epic-ability.

All Favor amounts are listed for completing the quest on Elite difficulty. All tables are sortable by Quest Name, Quest Level, Total Favor, Patron, Experience (Casual/Normal/Hard/Elite), and whether or not the quest offers Epic-level play.

Tempest's Spine 10 - - Free To Play No flagging required.
Plane of Night 10 22 - Vault of Night Complete The Vault of Night. You can only enter this raid from within that one.
The Vault of Night 10 22 - Vault of Night Vault of Night (story arc): Tharashk Arena (VoN1), The Prisoner (VoN2), Gateway to Khyber/The Jungle of Khyber (VoN3), and Haywire Foundry (VoN4).
Legendary Tempest's Spine - - 32 Free To Play No flagging required.
Riding the Storm Out - - 32 Dragonblood Prophecy No flagging required.
The Twilight Forge 11 - - The Restless Isles A completed Sigil of Dal Quor. Having someone on hand who can cast Greater Teleport will also save you a run through the wilderness.
Ascension Chamber 17 - - The Necropolis, Part 4 A completed Sigil: Frame and the four paths of the Litany of the Dead pre-raid quest.
The Chronoscope 6 21 - Devil Assault No flagging required.
The Reaver's Fate 14 - - Ruins of Gianthold Complete Madstone Crater, Prison of the Planes, and A Cabal for One, then Gianthold Tor.
A Vision of Destruction 18 - - The Vale of Twilight No flagging required.
The Master Artificer 19 - - Secrets of the Artificers No flagging required.
Fire Over Morgrave - - 34 Vecna Unleashed No flagging required.
Legendary Hound of Xoriat - - 32 Free To Play No flagging required.
Old Baba's Hut - - 32 Mists of Ravenloft No flagging required. However, unless your soul is currently bound to a spirit binder in one of the three public areas in Barovia, you must complete Into the Mists once on your current life to get to the entrance.
The Codex and the Shroud - - 32 The Vale of Twilight Complete To Curse the Sky and Creeping Death in Meridia.
Den of Vipers - - 36 Free To Play No flagging required.
Legendary Master Artificer - - 32 Secrets of the Artificers No flagging required.
The Chronoscope (Legendary) - - 32 Devil Assault No flagging required.
Hunt or Be Hunted - - 32 Hunter and Hunted No flagging required.
The Mark of Death - - 30 The Necropolis, Part 4 Complete the epic versions of Desecrated Temple of Vol, Flesh Maker's Laboratory, Inferno of the Damned, and Ghosts of Perdition.
Legendary Lord of Blades - - 32 Secrets of the Artificers No flagging required.
Caught in the Web - 24 - Menace of the Underdark All Menace of the Underdark quests.
Defiler of the Just - - 30 Trials of the Archons The Archons' Trial, Demon Assault, The Devil's Details
Temple of the Deathwyrm - - 30 Shadow Under Thunderholme Defeat Sarva Bellistrae in The Ruins of Thunderholme.
The Fall of Truth - 25 - Ruins of Gianthold Speak to Draznok ar'Athad, complete Return to Madstone Crater, Return to Prison of the Planes, and Return to Cabal for One, then complete Return to Gianthold Tor and finally speak to Draznok again.
Project Nemesis - - 32 Masterminds of Sharn No flagging required. The quest entrance is in Alcorin's Forge - Staging Area; if you have not completed all quests in Masterminds of Sharn Part One and Part Two, you will not be able to teleport to it. You may therefore have to leave your group to run through The Cogs.
Threats Old and New - - 35 Magic of Myth Drannor No flagging required.
Skeletons in the Closet - - 34 The Isle of Dread No flagging required. However, unless you have an expansion teleport item or your soul is bound in the Village of Tanaroa, you must enter Captive of the Hidden God and run through a small section of the Isle of Dread to get to the entrance if you haven't completed the quest this life. (You do not need to complete the quest, as a door in the entrance area leads straight to the wilderness.)
Fire on Thunder Peak - - 30 Shadow Under Thunderholme Defeat Dagan in The Ruins of Thunderholme.
The Curse of Strahd - - 32 Mists of Ravenloft Complete the following chains in order (quests within chains can be completed in any order):
In the Shadow of the Castle (Death House, Fresh-baked Dreams, and An Invitation to Dinner)
The Vampire Hunters (Oath of Vengeance, A Raven at the Door, A Mad Tea Party, and Sealed in Amber)
The Light of the Land (The Final Vintage, Wrath of the Earth, Raven's Bane, and Sunrise)
Unless your soul is bound in a Ravenloft public area, you must complete Into the Mists before anything else to reach the rest of the pack.
Too Hot to Handle - - 32 Masterminds of Sharn Complete all quests from Masterminds of Sharn Part One and Part Two. The Cogs quests are not required.
The Dryad and the Demigod - - 32 Fables of the Feywild No flagging required. If you have not visited Wynwood Hall before on your current life, you will need to run there through the wilderness, unless your soul is bound there.
Relentless - - 36 The Chill of Ravenloft No flagging required. However, unless your soul is bound in Ludendorf City Hall, you must complete The Body Snatchers to get to the entrance if you haven't completed the quest this life.
Tower of Despair 20 - - The Devils of Shavarath A New Invasion, Bastion of Power, Genesis Point, & Sins of Attrition.
Boots of Anchoring, The Banhammer or items with many charges of Spell Absorption are pretty much vital to avoid being kicked out of the end fight.
Visiting the entrance beforehand and speaking to the NPC in the tunnels to upgrade your Amrath portal will save you a run through the wilderness.
Hound of Xoriat 18 - - The Vale of Twilight No flagging required.
The Titan Awakes 12 - - The Restless Isles Complete The Twilight Forge. You can only enter this raid from within that one.
The Lord of Blades 20 - - Secrets of the Artificers No flagging required.
Zawabi's Revenge 12 22 - Demon Sands Complete Tomb of the Wizard-King, An Offering of Blood, and Chains of Flame, then Against the Demon Queen. As of Update 50 the ADQ1 pre-raid only needs to be repeated once per life.
Legendary Vision of Destruction - - 32 The Vale of Twilight No flagging required.
Terror of the Demon Lords - - 37 Terror of Demogorgon No flagging required.

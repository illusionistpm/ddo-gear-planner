The Sinister Secret of Saltmarsh

An evil Alchemist's mansion stands alone on a cliff and mysterious lights and hauntings have kept people away despite rumors of great treasure.

Sinister Secret of Saltmarsh is DDO's 6th expansion (first mini-expansion), introduced in Update 50, released August 4, 2021.

Features[edit]

  • Locations: Saltmarsh wilderness
  • Universal Enhancement Tree: Horizon Walker enhancements
  • Loot: Update 50 named items
  • Monsters: Bullywug, Crocodile, Nightmare, Water Weird

Adventure pack[edit]

  • The Sinister Secret of Saltmarsh 5-quest story arc (level 3/32) set in the Saltmarsh
  • 5 side quests (3/32) set in the Saltmarsh
  • Saga: Sinister Secret of Saltmarsh (Heroic) & Sinister Secret of Saltmarsh (Legendary)

Public zones[edit]

  • The Snapping Line Inn
  • Saltmarsh Training Hall

Wilderness adventure areas[edit]

  • Saltmarsh, a level 3/32 wilderness adventure area

Guest dungeon masters[edit]

  • Debra Wilson and Arthur Romeo

Quests[edit]

To access the area, go to The Gatekeepers' Grove in the Harbor, talk to NPC Val Arkalamnash and get transported to The Snapping Line Inn. Quest givers and entrance to the wilderness area is located within the inn. 4 main quests in the original AD&D modules in order are:

  • The Haunting of Saltmarsh
  • Under the Cover of Darkness
  • Danger at Dunwater
  • The Final Enemy

DDO story arc considers Swim at Your Own Risk as part of main quest also. All others are side quests and walkups.

Saltmarsh Items[edit]

  • Heroic and legendary items drop in chests.
  • You can convert a heroic item to its epic version using Altar of Epic Rituals in the Twelve (Epic Crafting).
  • You can convert an epic item to its legendary version using Altar of Legendary Rituals in the Twelve (Legendary Crafting).
  • Artifacts drop in legendary chests and as the legendary saga reward.
  • Augments drop in their appropriate chests.

Bundles and prices[edit]

Sinister Secret of Saltmarsh can be bought bundled with other features for cash money (3 different options available, as shown) in the DDO Market, or the Adventure Pack (adventures only) and/or many of those "other features", all separately, can be bought using DDO Points.

Talk to Garthalmor to claim your bonus items (located in The Snapping Line Inn in Saltmarsh) or in the Eberron Hall of Heroes.

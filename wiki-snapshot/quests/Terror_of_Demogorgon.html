Terror of Demogorgon is DDO's 10th expansion introduced in Update 81 and released July 22, 2026. It is based on the later sections of Out of the Abyss, a D&D 5E hardcover module released in 2015. The module is set in the Forgotten Realms, largely localised in the Underdark, and features adventurers going up against a number of Demon Princes freed into the Underdark from their prisons in the Abyss, culminating in a great battle with the terrible Demon Prince Demogorgon.

Features[edit]

  • Locations: The Darklake (wilderness)
  • Level cap: increase to 36
  • Race: Duergar race
  • Iconic Hero: Duergar Mindcleaver Iconic fighter
  • Mycella: Myconid Druid warlock, heals friends and hits enemies with Piercing DoTs
  • New cosmetic option: Teleportation Style Abyssal Door
  • Loot: Update 81 named items
  • Monsters: Babaus, Barlguras, Bulettes, Drakes, Giant Lizards, Moon Elves, Myconids

Adventure Pack[edit]

  • Terror of Demogorgon Part One: a 5-quest story arc (level 11/37)
    • Demon in the Rough • Tower of Vengeance • A Blood Pact • Hideous to Behold • The Price of an Egg
  • Terror of Demogorgon Part Two: a 5-quest story arc (11/37)
    • For Want of a Heart • Flocked Together • The Fetid Wedding • Stealing from Sorcere • Back to the Abyss
  • 3 side quests (11/37)
    • A Long Way to Mushrooms • If It Wasn't For Bad Luck • Raiding the Raiders
  • Raid: Terror of the Demon Lords
  • 2 Sagas: Terror of Demogorgon (Heroic) & Terror of Demogorgon (Legendary)

Public Zone[edit]

  • Gravenhollow

Wilderness Adventure Area[edit]

  • The Darklake – with support for all difficulty levels (no RXP)

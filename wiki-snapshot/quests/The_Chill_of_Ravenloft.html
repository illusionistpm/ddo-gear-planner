The Chill of Ravenloft is DDO’s 10th expansion introduced in Update 75 and released August 20th, 2025.

Features[edit]

  • Access the pack in the House Jorasco Enclave or Eveningstar across from the Into the Mists quest.
  • Locations: Land of Lamordia (wilderness)
  • Race: Dhampir race
  • Iconic Hero: Dhampir Dark Bargainer Iconic warlock
  • Drossheart: Alchemist hireling, level 8 and level 32. Uses throwing dagger in combat. Panacea and Melt Lock.
  • New feat category: Dark Gifts
    • One per character - unlocked by completing the Heroic saga, upgraded by completing the Legendary saga.
    • Dark Bargainers gain a free Dark Gift at creation but cannot upgrade it - they may then take and upgrade a second one.
  • New cosmetic option: Mistwalker Movement Style
  • Loot: The Chill of Ravenloft loot
  • Monsters: Sea Bird, Poltergeist, Brain in a Jar, Gremishka, Green Slime, Lamordian Flesh Golem, Frost Worm, Grick, Coldlight Walker, Gallows Speaker

Adventure pack[edit]

  • The Chill of Ravenloft Part One: a 5-quest story arc (level 8/36)
  • The Chill of Ravenloft Part Two: a 6-quest story arc (8/36)
  • 2 side quests (8/36)
  • Raid: Relentless
  • Saga: The Chill of Ravenloft (Heroic) & The Chill of Ravenloft (Legendary)

Public zone[edit]

  • Ludendorf City Hall

Wilderness adventure area[edit]

  • Land of Lamordia, a level 8/36 wilderness adventure area

Time line[edit]

  • December 2024: First leaked in December 2024, then
  • February 2025: Announced in the February 2025 producer letter
    • Description from the producer letter (February 2025):

This summer we're headed to Ravenloft with a new expansion set in Lamordia! Lamordia features the distinct "Victorian" style horror theme Ravenloft is famous for and introduces a new playable character race called the Dhampir, who are poised between the worlds of the living and the dead. Story-wise, players will discover who has been working to free Dark Lords from their Ravenloft domains and why. Dark Lords breaking loose is a big problem! We know our players are no strangers to extra-planar threats, and are up to the task.

  • June - Dhampir and Dark Bargainer Warlock preview.
  • June 19, 2025 -The Chill of Ravenloft available for pre-purchase.
  • July - Lamannia preview of Loot and a few quest previews.
  • August - Continuation of Lamannia preview of loot and quest previews.
  • August 20, 2025 - Release of The Chill of Ravenloft with Update 75
  • November 6, 2025 - Release of the raid Relentless with Update 75 Patch 7
  • January 2026 - The Chill of Ravenloft available in DDO Store.

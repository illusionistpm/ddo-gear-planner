Vecna Unleashed is DDO’s 8th expansion (2nd mini-expansion) introduced in Update 61 and released August 16th, 2023.

Features[edit]

  • Locations: Additional area added to Sharn, Morgrave University - Upper Commons
  • Epic Destiny: Machrotechnic
  • Loot: Update 61 named items
  • Monsters: Nothics, Wererats, Vrocks, and Balors

Adventure pack[edit]

  • Vecna Unleashed Part One - a 3-quest story arc (level 18/34)
    • Turn the Page • Grand Theft Aureon • Vecna Unleashed
  • Vecna Unleashed Part Two - a 6-quest story arc (18/34)
    • The Bark and the Blade • Enemy of My Enemy • The Evil We Know • Law and Order • Taken in Hand • Vecna Denied
  • 4 side quests (18/34)
    • Party 101 • Paper Trail • The Devils to Pay • What Dreams May Come
  • Raid: Fire Over Morgrave
  • Saga: Vecna Unleashed (Heroic) & Vecna Unleashed (Legendary)

Public zone[edit]

  • Morgrave University - Upper Commons

  • Talk to Makraal to claim your bonus items (located past City of Sharn Clifftop District, near the Airship to Morgrave University) or in the Eberron Hall of Heroes

Timeline[edit]

  • First announced in the January 2023 producer letter.
  • Prepurchase started on July 26, 2023.
  • Pre-order package became available with Update 60.
  • Released on Wednesday, August 16th, 2023 with Update 61.
  • Vecna Unleashed quest pack became available in the DDO Store March 2024.
